What it does

CourtConnect puts every athletic space at SDSU in one reservation system: basketball and volleyball courts, indoor soccer, football and soccer fields, pool lanes, the boxing gym and the yoga gym. Students book a time slot, show up, and check in. Clubs and organizations can book larger spaces for events and post them to a shared events feed.

Getting started

  1. RegisterSign up with your SDSU student ID and email.
  2. Pass the facility quizFill out the access form and pass a short quiz on safety rules and facility etiquette. You can't reserve until you pass.
  3. Reserve a spacePick a facility and an open time slot inside a reservation window.
  4. Check in when you arriveCheck in at the facility to confirm you used the reservation. If you don't, it counts as a no-show.

Booking rules

The rules keep spaces fair to share and discourage booking a court you won't use.

1 per day
Each student can hold one reservation per day.
Set windows
Reservations are only taken at set times of day. Outside those windows, spaces are first come, first served.
1 no-show
A temporary lockout from reserving.
3 no-shows
A permanent lockout from the app.

Clubs & organizations

Clubs, fraternities, sororities and other student orgs can reserve larger spaces, like a full field or gym, for events without going through the school directly.

Orgs can also post and promote events on the app, for example "Spikeball Club hosting a session at 1 pm on Ens Field." That turns CourtConnect into a light events and discovery feed on top of the booking system.

Database design

Six tables hold everything. Users make reservations for facilities; organizations host events at facilities; payments are recorded only for weekend or public reservations.

Users

  • student_idPK
  • name
  • email
  • rolestudent, club, public
  • statusactive, warned, banned
  • no_show_count

Facilities

  • facility_idPK
  • name
  • typecourt, field, pool lane, gym
  • capacity
  • location

Reservations

  • reservation_idPK
  • user_idFKUsers
  • facility_idFKFacilities
  • date
  • time_slot
  • statusbooked, checked-in, no-show, cancelled

Organizations

  • org_idPK
  • org_name
  • typeclub, frat, sorority
  • contact_user_idFKUsers

Events

  • event_idPK
  • org_idFKOrganizations
  • facility_idFKFacilities
  • date
  • time
  • description

Payments

  • payment_idPK
  • user_idFKUsers
  • amount
  • date
  • reservation_idFKReservations

Only created for weekend or public use.
