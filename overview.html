Product overview & instructions

What it does

CourtConnect puts every athletic space at SDSU in one reservation system: basketball, volleyball, pickleball, tennis and racquetball courts, turf fields, pool lanes, climbing walls, fitness studios and bowling lanes. Students book a 2-hour slot, show up, and check in. Clubs and organizations can book larger spaces for events and post them to a shared events feed.

Getting started

  1. RegisterSign up with your RedID and SDSU email.
  2. Pass the facility quizFill out the access form and pass a short quiz on safety rules and facility etiquette. You can't reserve until you pass.
  3. Reserve a spacePick a facility and an open 2-hour slot inside a reservation window, up to 7 days ahead.
  4. Check in when you arriveCheck in from 15 minutes before your slot until it ends. If you don't, it counts as a no-show.

Booking rules

The rules keep spaces fair to share and discourage booking a court you won't use.

1 per day
Each student can hold one reservation per day.
Set windows
Reservations are taken from 7 to 11 am and from 3 to 9 pm. From 11 am to 3 pm every space is first come, first served.
1 no-show
A temporary lockout from reserving.
3 no-shows
A permanent lockout from the app.

Clubs & organizations

Clubs, fraternities, sororities and other student orgs can reserve larger spaces, like a full field or gym, for events without going through the school directly.

Orgs can also post and promote events on the app, for example "Spikeball Club hosting a session at 1 pm on ENS 700 Field." That turns CourtConnect into a light events and discovery feed on top of the booking system.

Database design

Seven tables hold everything. Users make reservations for facilities; each facility has weekly bookable hours; organizations post events, each held on one reservation; and no-shows are recorded as penalties.

users

  • user_idPK
  • sdsu_red_idunique
  • emailunique
  • full_name
  • rolestudent, club, admin
  • statusactive, warned, banned
  • safety_quiz_passed
  • no_show_count
  • suspended_until
  • created_at

facilities

  • facility_idPK
  • name
  • typecourt, field, pool, gym, studio, other
  • location_area
  • capacity
  • is_active

facility_schedules

  • schedule_idPK
  • facility_idFKfacilities
  • day_of_week0 = Sunday
  • bookable_start
  • bookable_end

reservations

  • reservation_idPK
  • user_idFKusers
  • facility_idFKfacilities
  • start_time
  • end_time
  • check_in_time
  • statusbooked, checked-in, no-show, cancelled
  • created_at

organizations

  • org_idPK
  • name
  • categoryclub, fraternity, sorority
  • contact_user_idFKusers

events

  • event_idPK
  • reservation_idFKreservations
  • org_idFKorganizations
  • title
  • description
  • is_public_joinany student can join

penalties

  • penalty_idPK
  • user_idFKusers
  • reservation_idFKreservations
  • issued_at
  • reason
