Book the court before you get there.

CourtConnect is a campus app that lets SDSU students reserve any athletic space: courts, fields, pool lanes and gyms. The current ARC app doesn't do that.

See how it works Read about the project

A CS 514 database project at San Diego State University.

Every space on campus, on one schedule.

Right now you walk to the ARC and hope a court is free. CourtConnect shows what's booked, what's open, and where clubs are hosting events, so you can plan your day around it.

Sign up to reserve a space

The schedule is open to SDSU students. Create an account with your RedID and SDSU email, then pick a space and a time.

  • Courts, fields, pools, lanes and studios across campus
  • One reservation per day, free on weekdays
  • Check in when you arrive to keep your booking
