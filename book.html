CourtConnect is open to SDSU students. Sign up with your RedID and SDSU email, take a three-question safety quiz, and you're ready to book.

  • 7 days ahead you can book
  • Reservation windows. 7 to 11 am and 3 to 9 pm. From 11 am to 3 pm every space is walk-up.
  • Check in. From 15 minutes before your slot until it ends.
  • No-shows. One locks booking for 7 days. Three end it for good.
