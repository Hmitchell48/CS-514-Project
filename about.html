Who we are

CourtConnect is a hypothetical campus-tech company started by a team of SDSU computer science students for CS 514. We play pickup basketball, swim laps and run club events on campus, and we kept running into the same problem: there is no way to know if a space is free until you're standing in front of it.

So we designed the database and app that would fix it.

Mission

Make every athletic space at SDSU as easy to book as a study room, and fair to share between students, clubs and the wider community.

The problem

SDSU's ARC app only handles racquetball reservations, and it's limited to pickleball and badminton. Everything else has no reservation system at all.

Today

  • No way to reserve basketball or volleyball courts, indoor soccer, football or soccer fields, pool lanes, the boxing gym or the yoga gym.
  • Students show up and hope a space is free.
  • Clubs, frats and sororities have to go through the school to book a larger space for an event.
  • There's nowhere to find out what's happening on campus fields and courts.

With CourtConnect

  • Every sport space on campus is bookable from one app.
  • You know before you leave that your court or lane is held for you.
  • Orgs reserve larger spaces themselves and post their events.
  • A light events feed shows what's on, like a Spikeball Club session at 1 pm on Ens Field.

Value to users

Students

Guaranteed time on the space you want, free on weekdays. Fair rules mean one person can't hog a court: one reservation per student per day, and reservation windows at set times so the rest of the day stays first come, first served.

Clubs and organizations

Book a field or gym for an event without an approval chain, and reach students who might want to join through the events feed.

The university

Better use of facilities it already has, fewer no-shows thanks to check-in and penalties, and a path to new revenue from weekend fees and, eventually, public access.
