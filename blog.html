Riley Kenela

Front end

With this project, we aim to tackle the frustration students face trying to stay active on campus, like showing up to courts or fields only to find out they’re already occupied, reserved, or even locked. CourtConnect solves this problem by taking an existing system (or lack of one) that is limited and often not accurate and turning it into a real-time tracking system. What makes our system unique is not just the upscaling of a glorified calendar, but the inclusion of live tracking and updates to the system as well as accountability features like transparent reservation windows and automated no-show penalties to ensure popular spaces aren’t hogged by one group or left empty by people booking and not showing up. CourtConnect bridges the gap between recreational athletes, club events, and formal events in a single space, removing the guess work and allowing students to utilize their limited time on their actual events, and less time wasted on the coordinating.

Hudson Mitchell

Back end

This project aims to streamline the process for students and faculty to reserve SDSU ARC facility amenities, from courts to pool and bowling lanes. The existing first-come, first-served process can cause friction and unnecessary wait times. Students do not always know the state of the facility they wish to attend before making a trip there. CourtConnect will make the process much easier and stress-free, allowing students to plan their day and other work and school obligations around their reservation.

Yosef Pilip

Project lead

We decided to do this project because we saw something that was missing from SDSU's campus that could elevate the experience of many and bring people together. CourtConnect brings sports straight to your phone with info about not only the location of each court, field, pool, and other recreational facilities on campus, but it also tells you whether they are available or not and if any events are happening there. CourtConnect makes it easy for friends and groups of people to easily reserve recreational places to practice whatever sport they choose. It brings a platform to the school that allows clubs to advertise their meets to increase social connections, allows people to hold pickup games, notifying anyone in the school to come whether they are near or far, and provides an easy platform for big organizations like fraternities and sororities to easily reserve spaces for large events without the long email chains to the school's administration. A unique application that brings people together with ease and makes the school a little more technically advanced and brings campus-wide connection to a large school.

CourtConnect, a class project for CS 514 at San Diego State University.
